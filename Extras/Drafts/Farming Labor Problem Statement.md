# Farming Labor Problem Statement

The rural to urban migration of young adults has created a gap in legacy farming. It has reduced the available labor force for harvest work and other seasonal tasks. The problem is compounded by limited expertise and weak market understanding among farmers, which can force losses through bad harvests or low-price local sales.
